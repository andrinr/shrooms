02 / THE METHOD

A field guide for the
curious, not a crystal ball.

The 0–100 score is a relative habitat signal, not a calibrated probability. It blends the selected mushroom’s preferred habitat with the season and recent local weather. We show each ingredient so you can judge it yourself.

01Forest characterIndicative tree mix and habitat for each area.
02Moisture & rainOpen-Meteo soil moisture and recent precipitation.
03Season & temperatureSpecies season and recent air temperature.

Weather is modeled at an area scale, so it cannot describe a particular patch of forest. If live weather is unavailable, the score uses habitat and season only and is labelled accordingly.

03 / BEFORE YOU GO

Forage thoughtfully.

In Zürich, the first ten days of every month are a closed season for mushroom collecting. The daily limit is 1 kg per person, and collection is prohibited in nature reserves.
